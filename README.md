Braceplace Orthodontics Homepage Redesign
Website Overview
Braceplace Orthodontics (trading as Brace Place) is a private specialist orthodontic practice based in Hendon, North London. The practice provides personalised orthodontic treatments for children, teenagers and adults, led by specialist orthodontist Dr Adel Bou Saba.

The redesigned homepage focuses on presenting the practice's specialist orthodontic expertise, showcasing its treatment options and making it easier for prospective patients and families to understand the services and enquire about appointments.
Main Improvements
1. Clearer Specialist Positioning and Messaging
- Strengthened the homepage messaging to communicate the practice's specialist orthodontic focus.
- Improved the presentation of Dr Adel Bou Saba's clinical expertise and the practice's tailored approach to treatment.
- Made the value proposition clearer for adults, teenagers and parents seeking orthodontic care.
2. Better Treatment Organisation
- Improved the presentation of key orthodontic treatments, including Invisalign, fixed braces, Damon braces and lingual braces.
- Organised supporting dental services into a clearer structure.
- Made treatment information easier for visitors to scan and understand, helping them identify relevant options.
3. Improved Patient Enquiry Journey
- Strengthened the visibility of contact and appointment enquiry pathways.
- Made essential contact information, including telephone and WhatsApp details, easier to locate.
- Improved the flow between treatment information, practice credentials and contact opportunities to support patient decision-making.
Design Improvements
- Visual Presentation: Introduced a more refined and professional appearance aligned with the practice's specialist orthodontic positioning.
- Layout: Improved content hierarchy and section organisation to create a clearer homepage structure.
- User Experience: Made important treatment and contact information easier to discover.
- Consistency: Improved the presentation of treatment categories, clinical information and supporting content.
- Overall Appearance: Created a more cohesive homepage experience focused on clinical credibility, treatment clarity and patient enquiries.
