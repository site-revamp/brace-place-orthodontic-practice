Welcome to Brace Place

Specialist care. A more personal experience.

Choosing orthodontic treatment is a personal decision. At Brace Place, the focus is on understanding each patient's needs and helping them explore a treatment approach that suits their smile, age and goals.

Based in Hendon, the practice provides private orthodontic care for children and adults, alongside additional dental services. From your first conversation to the details of your treatment, the aim is to make the experience clear, considered and welcoming.
